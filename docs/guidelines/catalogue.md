# UK-first catalogue of radiology guidelines and classification systems

**Read first:** `frequency` is an estimate. `frequency_basis` starts with "Cited, not checked" (a named source recalled from memory, not re-read) or "Estimate" (reasoning only). Only rows with `verified: true` had their edition, year and URL confirmed against a live primary page or journal record in this task.

Authoring-order input for the offline guideline library. `catalogue.json` is the source of truth (one row per system; fields pinned by `backend/tests/test_library_catalogue.py`).

## Verification status

- Rows: 107 (32 UK bodies, listed first). Verified: 94. Unverified: 13.
- Method: issuer pages (NICE, BTS, RCR, RCOG, gov.uk, ACR, WHO, ESUR, NHS England) were fetched live and checked for the document title and publication/update date. Journal-based systems were checked against the Crossref record for the DOI (title, year, journal, volume, pages) and the DOI URL is the row url. NICE dates are the Published and Last updated dates read from each guidance page.
- `year` is the year of the edition named in `edition` (for NICE, the last-updated year).
- Unverified rows (`verified: false`) have either no located primary URL (`url` is empty and `licence_notes` says "URL not yet located") or an edition not confirmed live. The iRefer row is unverified because the site does not state its edition without login.
- Rows dropped from the first pass: society home-page index rows (BSTI, BSUR, BSNR, BSSR, BSGAR), RCR research-incidental, NHSBSP R1-R5 (no primary page found), NIPE hip US, UK breast U1-U5, ESR incidentalomas (could not be located), WHO bone tumour, AJCC 8, Hunt-Hess, Siewert, MSK-RADS, and duplicate-URL rows.
- Corrections found: RCR reporting standards is now a third edition (6 Oct 2025); RCR contrast standards 3rd edition is archived and replaced by new guidance; BI-RADS is now a v2025 manual; LI-RADS US surveillance is v2024; the BSG pancreatic cyst row was replaced by the European 2018 guideline (the BSG page edition was not confirmable). The SRU simple adnexal cyst consensus exists as a 2010 original and a 2019 update (Radiology 293:359), so the 2019 citation in the first pass was correct.
