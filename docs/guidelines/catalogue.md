# UK-first catalogue of radiology guidelines and classification systems

Authoring-order input for the offline guideline library. Source of truth is `catalogue.json` (one row per system, exactly the fields the shape test checks).

## Method

- UK bodies first (RCR, BTS, NICE, BSGAR/BSTI/BSUR/BSNR/BSSR, NHS screening programmes, RCOG), then international systems used in UK practice.
- `tier_candidate` 1 means executable decision logic (thresholds, categories, intervals) that can be encoded; 2 means reference material or an index pointer.
- `frequency` is an estimate of how often a UK radiologist meets the system; `frequency_basis` names an incidence or prevalence source where one is known, otherwise states the reasoning (screening volume, modality volume).
- URLs point at the issuing body or the journal article. Several index rows (society site home pages) are placeholders for guidance that still needs a primary document located before authoring.
- Live-checked in this pass: BTS pulmonary nodule page, the European 2018 pancreatic cyst guideline, the RCR cross-sectional imaging 3rd edition page and the NHS lung screening standard protocol. All other editions and years are from the issuer or journal record as known and have NOT each been re-fetched; verify the edition before authoring a pack.

## Caveats

- Some rows are weaker than others: those whose `edition` says rolling, index or pointer need a specific primary document found.
- Frequency figures cited in `frequency_basis` are approximate and from memory of the named source; treat as estimates.
