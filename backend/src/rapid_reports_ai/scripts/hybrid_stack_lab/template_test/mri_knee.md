# Skill Sheet: MRI Knee

## Scan Context
Non-contrast MRI of the knee for pain, instability or suspected internal derangement. Findings are reported by compartment rather than by tissue type.

## Report Structure
SECTION CLINICAL INFORMATION | header: "Clinical information:" | role: history
SECTION TECHNIQUE | header: "Technique:" | role: technique
SECTION FINDINGS | header: "Findings:" | role: findings
SECTION OPINION | header: "Opinion:" | role: impression
