## For Hassan

1. DECISION: the paper has no MR "grade 0" and no management. Reports that say "grade 0" or attach follow-up to a grade will find nothing in the card. Confirm that is acceptable.
2. DECISION: grade 3 is defined as signal reaching an articular surface, yet two stage 3 (tear) segments did not reach the surface and were still graded 3. The card states both (def.g3, pit.grade3) without choosing between them.
3. The 6% arthroscopy false-positive rate is quoted from the authors' forthcoming study (reference 22), not from this series. Included in pit.arthro with that attribution; remove if unwanted.
4. Library addition (also the pit.scope sentence on other sequences, field strengths and younger or acutely injured knees, now marked as library inference): pit.nograde0's last sentence ("not graded here") and the aliases (bare "Stoller", "meniscal signal grade", "grade N meniscal signal") are how reports name the system, not text from the paper.
5. Arithmetic note, not in the entry: segments in stages 1 to 3 total 35 of 36, so one segment is implied stage 0, but the paper does not state it.
