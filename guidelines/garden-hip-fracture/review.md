## For Hassan
- DECISION: Garden 1961 gives no management or prognosis per stage, so this is a tier 2 card (standing ruling). The usual report usage (I and II undisplaced, III and IV displaced, with fixation versus arthroplasty by age and fitness) is later practice, not in this paper, and is deliberately absent. If you want that usage in the library it needs a primary source such as NICE NG124 (hip fracture) or an orthopaedic society guideline, as a separate pack.
- The paper's own term is "Stage I to IV"; "Garden type/grade" is common usage. The aliases carry all of them.
- The stage definitions come from the figure captions, since the classification sentence gives only the four labels. The independent read viewed Figs 6-9 and found them consistent with the captions.
- `status: "current"` kept (no body has superseded it), but the card describes a foundational 1961 description, not a validated grading with criteria.
- Library addition: finding_type is narrowed to "subcapital femoral neck fracture" (independent read) so it does not fire on basal or pertrochanteric reports; the paper covers subcapital fractures only, and the pitfall says so.
