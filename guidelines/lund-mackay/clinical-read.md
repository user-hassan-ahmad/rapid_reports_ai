# Clinical read: lund-mackay@1993.1 (tier 2 reference card)

Independent read against Lund VJ, Mackay IS, Rhinology 1993;31:183-184 (sources/27e87d20...pdf and .txt). Table 1 was read from the page-2 image. Read-only; nothing edited.

## Verdict

Accept with minor changes. Every definition, the grading table and the variants table match the source. Nothing clinical comes from outside it. No Critical findings. Two Important findings concern gaps and matching risk, not errors.

## Source checks

- Sinus grades (def.sinus, sum.1): source text p183 "0: no abnormality; 1: partial opacification; 2: total opacification". Match.
- OMC (def.omc): "0" not obstructed or "2" obstructed; Table 1 "OMC (0 or 2)". Match. "No intermediate 1" is a fair restatement.
- Totals (def.total, sum.2): 0-24, each side 0-12. Match. Arithmetic checked: 5 groups x 2 + OMC 2 = 12 per side. Only a five-group split (anterior and posterior ethmoid separate, as on the form) gives these maxima.
- def.sides: the page image shows R and L column headers over the Sinus systems block, with Maxillary, Anterior ethmoids, Posterior ethmoids, Sphenoid, Frontal, OMC and TOTAL beneath. Confirmed. The Anatomic variants block has no R/L columns, so variants appear to be recorded once per patient, not per side. The card does not say which, and that is fine.
- tab.grading: matches the form. The "Total" row's meaning is the card's restatement of the p183 text, because the form's TOTAL line is blank. Honestly flagged as Hassan item 4.
- tab.variants and def.variants: six variants, recorded 1 present or 0 absent, not scored. The text names "agger nasi pneumatization" and the form names "Agger nasi cells". The card uses both. Match.
- sum.3 and pit.nobands: the paper gives no bands, thresholds or management. True on a full read.
- pit.partial, sum.4, pit.secretion: match p183 paragraph 3 and Discussion ("over-diagnosing disease extent where secretion rather than mucosal disease produces sinus opacification").
- pit.evidence: 200 scans, two scorers, 98% correlation, "starting point for future discussion". Match.
- pit.notscored: surgical score 0-14 (0-7 per side) matches. The source says endoscopic appearances are "not included in the staging system". For symptoms and surgical score it is only implied, because they are separate form sections.
- pit.scope: "need for a staging system of non-neoplastic sinus disease"; "patients undergoing endoscopic sinus surgery". Match. The clause "so it does not stage tumours" is an inference.

## Findings

1. Important. Gaps in the source are not recorded as pitfalls. The paper does not say how to score a sinus that is absent or hypoplastic (it lists "absent frontal sinus" only as a variant), what separates "partial" from "total" opacification, what counts as OMC obstruction, or whether post-operative anatomy is scored. A report writer will meet all of these and must not fill them in from outside. Suggest one pit.undefined pitfall, worded as "the paper is silent on these". Do not add a convention.
2. Important. Generic aliases "CT sinus score", "sinus CT score" and "LMS" can collide with other CT sinus staging systems (the paper itself cites Gaskins and Kennedy) and with unrelated abbreviations. Suggest dropping these three, or making them match only alongside a Lund-Mackay token.
3. Minor. In tab.variants the "Absent frontal sinus" row reads "1 present, 0 absent". This is a double negative: 1 means the frontal sinus is absent. Reword to "1 = variant present (frontal sinus absent), 0 = variant not present".
4. Minor. pit.nobands advises "state the score rather than label it mild, moderate or severe". That is library guidance and not from the source. It is sensible and clearly framed as a consequence of the absence of bands. Keep it, and consider tagging it as a library note.
5. Minor. pit.scope "so it does not stage tumours" is an inference from the stated intent. The paper's Discussion mentions malignancy staging only as an analogy. Suggest "was not developed for tumours" in place of "does not stage tumours".
6. Minor. pit.evidence calls the 98% figure "reliability". The paper says "98% correlation of scores" between two scorers, one of them an author. It is inter-observer correlation only, with no validation against symptoms or outcome. Suggest saying so.
7. Minor. pit.notscored: the symptom score and surgical score are not explicitly excluded in the text. Their exclusion is inferred from their being separate form sections. The statement is safe and the locator is honest.
8. Minor. Hassan item 3 (ethmoids): the p183 text says "each sinus group" without listing the groups. The five-group list comes only from the form. Worth saying in the card that the enumeration comes from Table 1, not the prose.

## For Hassan items

1. Agree. No bands or management in the paper, so none on the card. Later-source cut-offs should go in a separate companion pack with its own primary source.
2. Agree. The paper never uses the name "Lund-Mackay", so the display name and aliases are library additions. But see finding 2 on the generic aliases.
3. Agree. The card follows Table 1 (five groups, max 10 plus OMC 2 = 12 per side). The arithmetic in the text only works this way.
4. Agree. The form's TOTAL line is blank, and the "Total" row restates the p183 text. This is declared correctly.

Gate 5 low scores (def.sides 0.12, tab.grading 0.39) are explained by the form image having no usable text layer. I confirmed both against the image.
