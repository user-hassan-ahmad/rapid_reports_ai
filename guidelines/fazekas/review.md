## For Hassan

1. Library note: the modern "Fazekas scale" (total or combined, 0 to 6 if summed, and its use for small-vessel disease or Fazekas 2 or 3 as "moderate or severe") is not in this paper. The card carries only the two separate ratings and says there is no combined score. DECISION: if reports use "Fazekas 2" alone or a summed grade, the card will not define it; confirm that is acceptable.
2. The paper's table merges PVH 0 and 1 into one row; kept merged, not split.
3. The source gives no management for any grade; none is added.
4. Alias list includes bare "Fazekas" and "PVH/DWMH grade"; these are library additions of how reports name the system.
