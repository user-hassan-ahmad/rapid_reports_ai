## For Hassan

1. DECISION (aliases only): the source is the AO/OTA 44A/B/C classification, not the Danis-Weber paper. The id and the aliases
   (Weber, Weber A/B/C, Danis-Weber) follow the brief, so a report line "Weber B" finds this card. The compendium itself
   never states the equivalence, and 44A/B/C are defined by infra-, trans- and suprasyndesmotic fibular injury, which is how
   Weber A, B and C are usually described. No equivalence is written as a card fact; the aliases are a library addition.
   If you want the card to say "AO 44B corresponds to Weber B" it needs the Danis (1949) / Weber (1972) source or an
   AO statement, which Hassan would have to supply.
2. The definitions of 44A/B/C paraphrase the type, group and subgroup titles. The source gives no prose definition of
   "infrasyndesmotic", "transsyndesmotic" or "suprasyndesmotic" or of how to judge the level on plain film.
3. The card lists the qualification letters that appear on the pages; it does not reproduce the figures.
4. The card is XR only, as the brief set. The compendium is not modality-specific.
